CFO OPENING REMARKS

Our consolidated revenue for the quarter grew 12.3% year-on-year, slightly ahead of
street estimates. Retail segment same-store sales growth came in at 9.1%, moderating
from the prior quarter as quick-commerce competition intensified in top metros. Digital
services ARPU improved to INR 203, supported by continued 5G upsell.

ANALYST Q&A

Analyst: Can you comment on refining margins given the recent crude price volatility?

CFO: Refining margins compressed by roughly 80 basis points quarter-on-quarter due to
weaker gasoline cracks. We expect this to partially normalize in the second half as
Middle East supply stabilizes, but we are not providing precise guidance given the
volatility.

Analyst: How should we think about leverage going into the green energy capex cycle?

CFO: Net debt-to-equity remains comfortable at 0.30. We intend to fund the majority of
green energy capex through internal accruals and do not anticipate a material increase
in leverage over the next two fiscal years.

Analyst: Any update on the tax dispute disclosed in the annual filing?

CFO: There is no material change. We continue to believe the probability of adverse
cash outflow is low, consistent with our prior disclosure.

CLOSING REMARKS

Management reiterated full-year guidance for double-digit EBITDA growth, while flagging
that near-term retail growth may moderate further before stabilizing in the fourth
quarter.
