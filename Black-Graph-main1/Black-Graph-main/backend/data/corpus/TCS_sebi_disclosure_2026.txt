SUBJECT: Large Deal Wins and Deal Pipeline Update

Tata Consultancy Services disclosed total contract value (TCV) bookings of USD 10.2
billion for the quarter, up from USD 9.4 billion in the prior-year quarter. Management
attributed the increase to renewed momentum in the manufacturing and retail verticals,
partially offsetting continued softness in North American discretionary BFSI spend.

The company also disclosed a definitive agreement to acquire a mid-sized cloud
engineering consultancy for an undisclosed sum, expected to close within the current
fiscal year subject to customary regulatory approvals. The transaction is not expected
to have a material impact on near-term earnings per share.

Attrition for the trailing twelve months was reported at 12.3%, within the company's
targeted band and consistent with sequential improvement over the past four quarters.

No material litigation, regulatory action, or going-concern matters were disclosed in
this filing.
