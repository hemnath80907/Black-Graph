MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Reliance Industries reported consolidated revenue growth of 14.8% year-on-year for
fiscal 2025, driven primarily by the retail and digital services segments. The energy
and petrochemicals segment showed moderate growth of 6.2%, impacted by softer refining
margins in the second half of the year.

The company's consolidated net debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.32 as of March 2025,
down from 0.41 the previous year, reflecting continued deleveraging following the
completion of the telecom infrastructure buildout cycle.

RISK FACTORS

1. Refining margins remain exposed to global crude price volatility and geopolitical
   supply disruptions, which could compress petrochemical segment profitability.
2. The retail segment faces intensifying competition from quick-commerce entrants,
   which may pressure same-store growth rates in metro markets.
3. Regulatory changes to spectrum pricing and tariff structures in the telecom
   sector could affect digital services margins in the coming fiscal year.
4. Currency depreciation against the US dollar increases the cost of imported
   crude feedstock and could compress near-term margins if hedges are insufficient.

CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND OUTLOOK

The board has approved continued investment in green energy and battery storage
manufacturing capacity, with commissioning of the first phase expected in late 2026.
Management reiterated guidance for double-digit consolidated EBITDA growth for fiscal
2026, contingent on stable refining spreads and continued retail footprint expansion.

CONTINGENT LIABILITIES

The company disclosed contingent liabilities of approximately INR 18,400 crore related
to ongoing tax assessment disputes and bank guarantees extended to subsidiaries. Legal
counsel has assessed the probability of material cash outflow from these disputes as
low based on precedent rulings in similar matters.
