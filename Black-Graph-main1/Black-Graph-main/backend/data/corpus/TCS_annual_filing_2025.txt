MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Tata Consultancy Services reported constant-currency revenue growth of 7.1% for fiscal
2025, with the BFSI vertical contributing the largest share of incremental growth.
Operating margin held steady at 24.6%, supported by continued utilization improvements
and a stable pyramid structure.

The company remains debt-free at the consolidated level, with total debt-to-equity of
0.02, consisting solely of lease liabilities under applicable accounting standards.

RISK FACTORS

1. Discretionary technology spending among North American BFSI clients has shown signs
   of deceleration, which could weigh on near-term deal bookings.
2. Wage inflation for niche digital and AI talent remains a margin pressure point,
   particularly in competitive geographies such as the United States and Europe.
3. Currency movements in the GBP and EUR pose translation risk given the company's
   European client concentration.
4. Visa policy changes in key onshore markets could increase delivery costs if
   onsite-offshore ratios need to shift.

CAPITAL ALLOCATION

The board approved a share buyback program alongside the existing dividend policy,
reflecting continued confidence in free cash flow generation. The company maintains a
policy of returning at least 80% of free cash flow to shareholders over a rolling
period, subject to board approval each year.

CONTINGENT LIABILITIES

Contingent liabilities of approximately INR 12,100 crore were disclosed, primarily
related to indirect tax matters across multiple jurisdictions. Management does not
consider any of these matters individually material to the consolidated financial
position.
