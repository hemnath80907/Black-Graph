CEO OPENING REMARKS

Revenue for the quarter grew 4.2% year-on-year in constant currency, at the lower end
of our guided range. Large deal TCV came in at USD 2.3 billion, down from USD 2.8
billion in the prior quarter, reflecting elongated decision cycles among enterprise
clients in North America and Europe.

CFO REMARKS ON MARGINS

Operating margin for the quarter was 21.1%, down 40 basis points sequentially due to
wage hikes effective this quarter and continued investment in generative AI delivery
capabilities. We expect margin to stabilize in the 20.5% to 22.5% band for the
remainder of the fiscal year.

ANALYST Q&A

Analyst: Are you seeing any signs of discretionary spend recovery?

CFO: We are seeing early, uneven signs of recovery in select verticals such as retail
and manufacturing, but BFSI discretionary spend in North America remains muted. It is
too early to call this a broad-based recovery.

Analyst: How is the debt and balance sheet positioned given the buyback program?

CFO: We remain effectively debt-free at the consolidated level. The current buyback
program is being funded entirely from existing cash and investment balances without
any incremental borrowing.

GUIDANCE

Management revised full-year constant-currency revenue growth guidance to a range of
3.5% to 5.0%, down from the previous range of 4.5% to 6.5%, citing continued
macroeconomic uncertainty in key client geographies.
